# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

This policy explains what Sonetto ("we", "us") collects when you use Quartz at quartz.graphics, why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

**Account details.** Your email address and password. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider; we never see them.

**Your content.** Decks, slides, text, images and files you upload, and thumbnails we generate of your slides.

**Payment details.** If you buy a paid plan, our payment provider collects your payment and billing details. We receive only what we need to manage your plan, such as your plan, billing status and country — never your full card number.

**Discord details.** If you use the Discord integration, Discord shares your Discord user ID, username and avatar with us.

**Technical data.** Like most websites, our servers log your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. We use this to keep the Service running and secure.

**Usage and performance data.** We use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to count page visits and measure how fast pages load. They record things like the page, referrer, country, device and browser type, and page load times. They don't use cookies and don't identify you personally.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't sell your data.

## Why we use it

- To run the Service: log you in, save and sync your decks, and show your presentations.
- To send emails you need, such as sign-up confirmation and password resets.
- To bill paid plans.
- To keep the Service secure and fix problems.
- To understand how Quartz is used and make it faster.
- To meet legal obligations.

## Cookies and browser storage

We use cookies to keep you logged in, and browser storage to cache things like image links so pages load faster. Both are needed for the Service to work.

## Who we share it with

We use these providers to run Quartz. They process data only to provide their service to us:

| Provider  | What for                                |
| --------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Supabase  | Database, login and file storage        |
| Vercel    | Hosting, analytics and speed insights   |
| Resend    | Sending email                           |
| Polar     | Payments                                |
| Discord   | Discord integration, only if you use it |
| Fontshare | Serving fonts                           |

We also share data if the law requires it, or to protect people or the Service. If Sonetto is sold or merged, your data may move to the new owner under this policy.

Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States and the European Union.

## Content you share

If you share or present a deck, the people you share it with can see its content. Anything you put in a shared deck is visible to them.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and content until you delete them or your account. Deleted data may stay in backups for a short time before it is erased. We keep billing records for as long as tax law requires.

## Your choices and rights

You can:

- see and edit your content in the app;
- delete your content or your account;
- ask us for a copy of your personal data, or to correct or delete it.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as objecting to how we use your data. Contact us to use any of them. You can also complain to your local privacy regulator — in Australia, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

## Security

We use encrypted connections and limit who can access data. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise your data will never be exposed. If a breach puts you at risk, we will tell you.

## Changes

If we make significant changes to this policy, we will tell you by email or in the app before they take effect.
